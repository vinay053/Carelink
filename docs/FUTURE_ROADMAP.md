# FUTURE_ROADMAP.md — Product Evolution & Feature Roadmap

## Phase 1: Near-Term Enhancements (Q4 2026)
- **Real-Time WebSockets**: Migrate from client-side polling to Socket.IO for instant referral stage transitions and emergency alerts.
- **ABDM Milestone 1–3 Integration**: Direct integration with the National Health Authority (NHA) gateway for ABHA creation, verification, and consent artifact management.
- **HL7 FHIR Bundle Export**: Full export of referral summaries and encounter histories into FHIR R4 JSON standard.

---

## Phase 2: Medium-Term Capabilities (Q1 2027)
- **Offline PWA Support**: Enable service workers, background sync, and local IndexedDB caching so healthcare workers in remote sub-centers can register patients and draft referrals without an active cellular connection.
- **Ambulance Fleet Live Telemetry**: Real-time GPS tracking of transit vehicles with automatic ETA updates for receiving emergency departments.
- **Multi-Lingual Clinical Translation**: Localization of patient notifications and referral sheets into Hindi, Marathi, and other regional Indian languages.

---

## Phase 3: Long-Term Vision (Q2–Q4 2027)
- **State-Wide Bed Census Integration**: Real-time integration with state healthcare ERP databases for automated bed reservation during emergency transfers.
- **Predictive Capacity Forecasting**: Machine learning models forecasting ICU and ventilator demand based on seasonal outbreak patterns and historical district referral velocity.
